Project overview · 2026

Barriers to Fresh Food Access

Community findings about grocery shopping, food security, health, and transportation in Syracuse, New York.

Syracuse UniversityFAHNNUniversity of California, Irvine

The project centers residents’ experiences to understand how household resources, neighborhood options, and transportation shape access to fresh food.

Community survey

The survey was conducted from October through December 2025. It produced 476 valid responses: 421 completed in person and 55 completed online. Each participant received a $25 gift card.

476

valid responses across the Syracuse community survey.

Three connected views

Nutritional health and wellness

Residents reported on food security, self-described health, stress, diet-related conditions, and whether fresh food could help address health concerns.

Grocery shopping overview

Residents described where they shop, what makes grocery shopping difficult, and which food retail and neighborhood options would improve fresh food access.

Grocery shopping by city bus

Residents shared how often they use the bus for groceries, barriers they encounter, safety concerns, and practical transportation improvements.
